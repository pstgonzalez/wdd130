Loa Rafting

Loa Rafting creates wonderful, safe rafting adventures full of excitement and adrenaline on the rivers of Pucón, Chile.

History

Loa Rafting is a tourism company operating throughout Chile, offering activities that range from relaxing experiences to adrenaline-fueled adventures. Loa Rafting Pucón is one of its branches born from a passion for the country's rivers and natural beauty and aims to provide memorable rafting experiences led by qualified professionals who value safety, teamwork, and respect for nature.
